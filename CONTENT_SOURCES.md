## Review considerations

The private preview retains links to the school’s actual external services. Tours use a mailto link, rather than a nonfunctional form. The original newsletter signup is not reproduced as an unconnected form. Calendar dates and tuition rates are a review snapshot, not a live synchronization. Reading totals come from the live tracker, with a visibly dated fallback if unavailable. Historical accreditation information is attributed to the school’s published history rather than independently asserting current accreditation status.
